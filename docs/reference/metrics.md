# Metrics

> **Status:** reference
> **As of:** d8f375bc

Every metric Scavengarr exports, with its labels, buckets and label values, and the endpoints that serve them.
How the core records them: [Observability](../current/observability.md).

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Content |
|---|---|
| `GET /metrics` | Every metric below in the Prometheus text format (0.0.4), rendered in a worker thread. |
| `GET /api/v1/stats/metrics` | JSON for a quick look, described under [The JSON statistics](#the-json-statistics). |
| `GET /api/v1/stats/plugins` | The plugins' long-term record, described under [The plugin record](#the-plugin-record). |

## Metric table

A histogram serves the samples `<name>_bucket`, `<name>_sum` and `<name>_count`.
A stage of `TelemetryPort` is two families: `scavengarr_<stage>_seconds` with its duration and `scavengarr_<stage>_total` with its outcome.
A label combination exists only once it occurred.
The gauges and the container's counter are read at scrape time.
The container's two families are absent outside a cgroup v2 container.
prometheus-client adds `process_*` (the Python process), `python_gc_*` and `python_info`, which the table leaves out.

The buckets follow the deadlines.
An answer goes out at the latest 60 s after the request, a plugin search ends 30 s after it, and a resolution gets 15 s.
A capture the resolve bound cut after a challenge runs on for at most 20 s.

## Label values

Label values come only from fixed sets: plugin names, resolver names, breaker keys and the outcomes below.
They never carry titles, IMDb ids, URLs, domains or stream ids.
An outcome in *italics* is counted without a duration (`count()`).
Without an outcome set by the code, a stage ends as `ok`, as `cut` on a cancellation, as `timeout` on a `TimeoutError` and as `error` on any other exception.

### Stremio requests

- `stremio_request` `source`: `cache` (a fresh cache entry), `stale` (a stale entry, refreshed in the background), `search` (a new search), `joined` (the title's search was running) or `none` (ended before the search).
- `stremio_request` `outcome`: `streams`, `empty`, `no_title`, `no_plugins`, `error` or `cut`.
- `stremio_streams` counts the streams per answer.

### Stremio phases

- `anime_ids`: a `kitsu:` id translated into the IMDb request, `found` or `not_found`.
- `series_meta`: the request's Cinemeta meta, `found`, `not_found` or `error`.
- `metadata`: the plugin selection, the TMDB titles and an IMDb anime episode's Kitsu number, `found` or `not_found`.
- `search`: the shared search, once per search, `ok` or `cut`.
- `resolve`: the resolution while the search runs, `target`, `done`, `budget` (the answer budget passed and the resolutions of the results known by then are done), `deadline` or *`cached`* (a cached answer).
- `background_resolve`: the other links of a cached answer, `done` or `deadline`.

### Plugin searches

- `plugin_search` records Stremio searches only; Torznab searches are not recorded.
- Its outcomes: `hits`, `empty`, `late`, `error`, `cut` (shutdown), *`breaker_open`* and *`unreachable`*.
- `late` is a search that returned after the request's answer budget: its results reach the cache and the next request.
- *`unreachable`* is a plugin that failed the periodic health check or found no reachable domain in this search.
- `plugin_results_total` counts the validated results.

### Hoster resolutions

- `hoster_resolve` `resolver`: the resolver's name, or `direct` for a content-type probe (a playlist, a URL without a resolver).
- Its outcomes: `stream`, `dead`, `unplayable`, `check_error` (the playback check failed), `timeout`, `challenge`, `network_error`, `http_error`, `error`, `cut` and `busy`.
- `challenge` is a resolution whose pages showed a challenge and found no stream while it stood, or that the bound cut after one; the breaker does not count it.
- `busy` is a capture that got no stealth browser page before its request was due.
- Its outcomes without a run: *`cached`*, *`breaker_open`*, *`challenge_cooldown`* (a link skipped while its hoster's challenge cooldown runs) and *`joined`* (a request for a link whose challenged capture runs on in the background waited for it instead of a second capture).
- `hoster_challenge_completion` times a capture the resolve bound cut after a challenge, run on in the background, from the cut to its end.
- Its outcomes: `stream` (judged and cached under its link), `none` (no stream, an error, or a stream the judgement refused) and `cancelled` (the 20 s deadline or shutdown).
- A `none` or a `cancelled` while the challenge stands steps the hoster's challenge cooldown up; no stream past the challenge is a dead link, cached as one.
- `hoster_pass_keep` times Filemoon's pass keeper: the capture of a cached Filemoon link once the kept captcha pass has gone, from its start to the resolution's end ([Filemoon's pass keeper](../current/hoster-resolvers.md#filemoons-pass-keeper)).
- Its outcomes: `stream`, `none` (no stream within the bound; a capture the bound cut after the captcha runs on as a `hoster_challenge_completion`), *`busy`* (no page stays free beside its own: it looks again 30 s later), *`no_link`* (no cached Filemoon stream) and *`idle`* (no request looked up a Filemoon link within 48 h).
- The pass keeper's capture counts as a `hoster_resolve` too.

### HLS proxy

- `hls_proxy` `kind`: `master` (the stream's playlist), `playlist`, `segment` or `file` (an address-bound direct file through the file proxy, mostly answered `206`).
- Its outcome is the answer's HTTP status, and its duration ends when the answer starts (the time to the first byte for the player).
- `hls_proxy_bytes_total` counts the bytes sent, an aborted transfer's bytes included.
- `hls_readahead` records the fetches of segments ahead of the player: `ok`, `failed`, `too_large` (over 16 MiB, left to the player), `throttled` (the CDN answered 429) or `cut` (dropped while in flight).
- It also counts the answers: `hit` (served from memory), `miss` (fetched on demand, also a segment no playback lists: an init segment, a key) and `dropped` (fetched ahead, never asked for, or swept 60 s after the playback's last request).
- `hit` over `hit` plus `miss` says how often the read-ahead was there in time.

### Stealth browser

- `browser_page_wait` and `browser_page` `kind`: `play` (a stream resolved again at play time), `plugin` (a plugin's page, also from Torznab searches and the scoring probes), `capture` (a hoster capture for an answer) or `background` (a capture for later requests).
- `browser_page_wait` outcomes: `ok` (got the page), `busy` (none came free 3 s before the work was due) or `cut`.
- Work without a due time, from Torznab searches and the scoring probes, waits instead of ending `busy`.
- `browser_page` times the work from the grant to its end: `ok`, `cut` or `error`.
- A capture's page that stays open after its stream went out, for its state's read, counts in `browser_pages{state="in_use"}` until it closes, outside the duration.
- `browser_pages` `state`: `limit`, `in_use` or `waiting`.
- `PageBudget` moves the limit and logs each change as `browser_pages_changed` with its reason (`wait`, `memory`, `cpu` or `idle`).

### Process and build

- `event_loop_lag_seconds`: CPU work on the event loop delays every timeout and deadline by as much as a timer fires late.
- `circuit_breaker_open` `breaker`: `plugin` or `hoster`; `state`: `open` or `half_open`; a plugin breaker's `name` is keyed `plugin:category`.
- The container's CPU minus `process_cpu_seconds_total` is Chromium, its driver, Xvfb and the health checks.
- `build_info` names the app version, the commit and the build time; without a recorded build the last two are `unknown`.
- The image build writes them to `build_info.json` beside the package (`docker/build_info.py`, from the `SCAVENGARR_COMMIT` and `SCAVENGARR_BUILT` build arguments or the checkout), and the environment variables of the same names override it ([Deploy](../how-to/deploy.md)).
- `/api/v1/healthz`, `/api/v1/stremio/health` and the startup line `app_startup_complete` report the same three values.

## The JSON statistics

`GET /api/v1/stats/metrics` returns:

- the plugin statistics, from the same metrics;
- the event-loop lag of the last 5 minutes (p50, p99, max);
- the plugin circuit breakers (the hoster breakers only in `/metrics`);
- `unresolved_hosters`: the 20 hosters probed most often for want of a resolver, an open set of names and so no Prometheus label ([Hoster resolvers](../current/hoster-resolvers.md#dispatch));
- `hoster_state`: what the start restored of the run before (`restored_at`, the snapshot's `age_s`, `resolutions`, `redirects`, `breakers`);
- the concurrency pool and the shutdown state.

## The plugin record

`GET /api/v1/stats/plugins` reports `today`, `window_days` and per plugin `last_result_day`, `unreachable_share` over `30`, `90` and `180` days (`null` without a check) and the `days` with their counters.
`PluginHistory` (`infrastructure/plugins/history.py`) counts per plugin and UTC day:

| Counter | Counted by | When |
|---|---|---|
| `searches` | `PluginSearchRunner` | A plugin search of a Stremio request that ran; a plugin skipped by its breaker or the health check is not counted. |
| `results` | `PluginSearchRunner` | The validated results a search gave. |
| `timeouts` | `PluginSearchRunner` | A search cut by the plugin timeout ([The Stremio search timing](../current/architecture.md#the-stremio-search-timing)). |
| `dropped` | `PluginSearchRunner` | The results of a series request the episode filter dropped: another season or episode, or a show page without the requested episode. |
| `challenges` | `PluginSearchRunner` | A search in which a page of the plugin's site showed a challenge, counted once per search. |
| `challenges_unsolved` | `PluginSearchRunner` | A search with a challenge the plugin did not get past, counted once per search; an older day without it reads 0. |
| `checks` | `PluginHealthMonitor` | A check of the plugin's site with a verdict; a check in which no site answered counts nothing. |
| `unreachable` | `PluginHealthMonitor` | A check that found the site down after the 30 s retry; `unreachable_share` divides them by `checks`. |
| `unreachable_searches` | `PluginSearchRunner` | A Stremio search in which the plugin's domain check found no domain. |

How the bases mark a challenge and when the health monitor checks: [Observability](../current/observability.md#the-plugin-record).
